# miRNA Biomarker Analysis Pipeline - Methods

## Overview

This document describes the computational methods used to identify tissue-specific miRNA biomarker candidates from small RNA sequencing (small RNA-seq) data. The pipeline takes as input a table of differentially expressed (DE) miRNAs generated by an upstream DE analysis tool (DESeq2-based) and produces a set of interactive visualizations and summary tables characterizing group-specific miRNA expression signatures.

### Experimental Groups

Six biological groups were analyzed, consisting of one cell line and five murine organ tissues:

| Group | Abbreviation | Replicates |
|-------|-------------|------------|
| NIH 4T1 cells | Cells | 3 (4T1.1, 4T1.2, 4T1.3) |
| Heart | He | 2 (He.4, He.5) |
| Kidney | Ki | 2 (Ki.4, Ki.5) |
| Liver | Li | 3 (Li.4, Li.5, Li.3) |
| Lung | Lu | 3 (Lu.4, Lu.5, Lu.3) |
| Spleen | Sp | 3 (Sp.4, Sp.5, Sp.3) |

---

## 1. Input Data

The input file (Updated 7066_DEmiRNAs.xlsx) contains the output of a differential expression analysis performed upstream. The sheet "signatures DE genes table" contains:

- **Gene-level information**: miRNA identifiers (GeneID), the number of pairwise comparisons in which each miRNA was tested, and the maximum observed absolute log2 fold change.
- **Six pairwise comparisons**, each structured as a one-vs-rest design:
  - He_vs_nonHe: Heart vs. all other groups
  - Ki_vs_nonKi: Kidney vs. all other groups
  - Li_vs_nonLi: Liver vs. all other groups
  - Lu_vs_nonLu: Lung vs. all other groups
  - Sp_vs_nonSp: Spleen vs. all other groups
  - Cells_vs_Organs: All organ tissues vs. Cells (note: group1 = Organs, group2 = Cells in the source data; the pipeline inverts the log2 fold change so that positive values indicate upregulation in Cells)
- **Per-comparison statistics** (for each of the six comparisons): average normalized counts for group1 and group2, log2 of the average expression, log2 fold change (log2FC), absolute log2FC, raw p-value, Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-value (FDR/padj), significance flag, minimum normalized count threshold, minimum log2FC threshold, and a binary differential expression flag.
- **Group average expression**: baseMean and per-group average normalized counts.
- **Individual sample counts**: normalized counts for each of the 16 biological samples.

### Data Parsing

The Excel sheet is parsed programmatically. Rows 1-5 are header/metadata and are skipped. Each comparison occupies 11 consecutive columns. Group average columns and individual sample count columns follow the comparison blocks. All numeric fields are coerced from their string representations, with non-numeric values treated as missing (NaN).

## 2. Volcano Plots

### Purpose
Volcano plots provide a global view of differential expression for each comparison, showing statistical significance against fold change magnitude. They allow rapid identification of miRNAs that are both highly significant and strongly differentially expressed.

### Method
For each of the six comparisons, miRNAs are plotted with log2 fold change on the x-axis and -log10(FDR) on the y-axis. Points are classified into four categories:

| Category | Criteria | Color |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Up | FDR <= 0.05 and log2FC >= 1.0 | Red |
| Down | FDR <= 0.05 and log2FC <= -1.0 | Blue |
| Sig (low FC) | FDR <= 0.05 and abs(log2FC) < 1.0 | Gray |
| Not Sig | FDR > 0.05 | Light gray |

Dashed reference lines are drawn at FDR = 0.05 (horizontal) and log2FC = +/-1.0 (vertical). The top 5 most significant upregulated and top 5 most significant downregulated miRNAs are labeled by name.

For the Cells_vs_Organs comparison, the log2FC values are negated prior to plotting so that positive values consistently represent upregulation in the focal group (Cells), matching the convention used by the other five comparisons.

### Outputs
- Six individual volcano plot HTML files (one per comparison).
- One combined volcano plot with a dropdown selector to switch between comparisons.

## 3. Heatmap of Top Differentially Expressed miRNAs

### Purpose
The heatmap provides a cross-group expression overview of the most broadly differentially expressed miRNAs, enabling visual identification of tissue-specific and shared expression patterns.

### Method
1. **Gene selection**: miRNAs are ranked by (a) the number of comparisons in which they are significantly DE (DIFF count, descending) and (b) their maximum absolute log2FC across all comparisons (descending, as a tiebreaker). The top 60 are selected.
2. **Expression normalization**: For each selected miRNA, the average normalized counts per group are log2-transformed (with a pseudocount of 1 added to avoid log(0)). Row-wise z-score normalization is then applied: z = (log2_expr - row_mean) / row_sd. This centers each miRNA expression at zero and scales by its standard deviation, allowing comparison of relative expression patterns across miRNAs with vastly different absolute expression levels.
3. **Ordering**: miRNAs are sorted by the group in which they have their maximum z-score, producing visual blocks of co-expressed miRNAs.
4. **Color scale**: RdBu_r (reversed Red-Blue diverging), centered at z = 0. Red indicates above-average expression; blue indicates below-average expression.

### Output
- heatmap_top_DE_miRNAs.html: Interactive heatmap with hover text showing the miRNA name, group, z-score, and raw average count.

## 4. Group-Specific Biomarker Identification

### Purpose
To identify miRNAs that serve as tissue-specific biomarkers: miRNAs whose expression is significantly and specifically enriched (upregulated) or depleted (downregulated) in one group relative to all others.

### Method
For each group, the corresponding one-vs-rest comparison is used to identify biomarker candidates. A miRNA is considered a biomarker candidate if it meets all three of the following criteria:

1. **Statistical significance**: Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p-value (FDR) <= 0.05.
2. **Effect size**: Absolute log2 fold change >= 1.0 (corresponding to a 2-fold change).
3. **Minimum expression**:
   - For **upregulated** candidates (log2FC > 0): the average normalized count in the focal group must be >= 10. This ensures the miRNA is detectably expressed where it is claimed to be enriched.
   - For **downregulated** candidates (log2FC < 0): the maximum average normalized count across the *other* groups must be >= 10. This ensures the miRNA is meaningfully expressed elsewhere, so its absence or reduction in the focal group represents a real biological signal rather than universal low expression.

### Biomarker Scoring

Each candidate is assigned a composite biomarker score that integrates three properties:

    biomarker_score = -log10(padj + 1e-300) * abs(log2FC) * clip(specificity, 0, 10)

Where:
- **-log10(padj)**: Rewards statistical confidence (higher = more significant).
- **abs(log2FC)**: Rewards larger effect sizes.
- **specificity**: Defined as the ratio of the focal group average expression to the overall baseMean (avg_group / (baseMean + 1)). Higher values indicate expression concentrated in the focal group. Clipped at 10 to prevent extreme outliers from dominating.

### Additional Metrics

- **Coefficient of Variation (CV)**: Calculated within the focal group biological replicates as SD / mean. Lower CV indicates more reproducible expression across replicates, suggesting a more reliable biomarker.
- **Direction**: Each candidate is labeled as "UP in [Group]" or "DOWN in [Group]" based on the sign of its log2FC.

### Outputs
- biomarker_candidates.csv: Top 20 candidates per group (ranked by biomarker score), including log2FC, FDR, group averages, direction, specificity, CV, and biomarker score.
- biomarker_summary_tables.csv: Top 10 upregulated and top 10 downregulated candidates per group, with average counts and z-scores across all six groups. This file enables direct comparison of a biomarker expression profile across tissues.

## 5. Expression Profile Dot Plots

### Purpose
Dot plots visualize the expression of top biomarker candidates across all groups simultaneously, using two visual encodings (dot size and color) to convey both expression magnitude and relative expression. Two separate plots are generated - one for upregulated and one for downregulated biomarkers - to ensure both types of tissue-specific signatures are visible.

### Method
1. **Gene selection**: For each group, the top 10 biomarker candidates are selected (by biomarker score) in the specified direction (upregulated: log2FC > 0; downregulated: log2FC < 0).
2. **Expression data**: Group-average normalized counts are retrieved for each selected miRNA across all six groups.
3. **Z-score normalization**: Log2-transformed average counts (pseudocount = 1) are z-score normalized per miRNA (across the six groups), identical to the heatmap method.
4. **Visual encoding**:
   - **Dot size**: Proportional to log2(average count + 1), scaled to a range of 3-30 pixels. Larger dots indicate higher absolute expression.
   - **Dot color**: Mapped to the z-score using a diverging RdBu_r color scale, clamped to [-3, +3]. Red indicates above-average expression for that miRNA; blue indicates below-average expression.

### Interpretation
- In the **upregulated** plot, a group's own biomarker candidates should appear as large red dots in that group's column (high expression, high z-score) and as smaller blue dots in other groups' columns.
- In the **downregulated** plot, a group's own biomarker candidates should appear as small blue dots (low or absent expression) in that group's column and as larger red dots in other groups where the miRNA is more highly expressed.

### Outputs
- biomarker_dotplot_upregulated.html: Top 10 upregulated biomarker candidates per group.
- biomarker_dotplot_downregulated.html: Top 10 downregulated biomarker candidates per group.

---

## 6. Individual Sample Expression Box Plots

### Purpose
Box plots show the distribution of normalized counts across individual biological replicates for top biomarker candidates, providing a view of within-group variability and between-group separation at the sample level.

### Method
For each group, the top 5 upregulated biomarker candidates (by biomarker score) are selected. For each miRNA, a grouped box plot is generated showing the distribution of normalized counts for each of the six groups. Individual data points are overlaid using strip/jitter plotting.

### Interpretation
A strong biomarker candidate should show clear separation between its focal group and all other groups, with low within-group variability (tight boxes).

### Output
- biomarker_expression_boxplots.html: Subplot grid of box plots, one per miRNA.

---

## 7. MA Plots

### Purpose
MA plots (Bland-Altman plots) display the relationship between overall expression level and fold change, helping to identify expression-dependent biases and to contextualize differential expression in terms of absolute abundance.

### Method
For each comparison, miRNAs are plotted with log2(mean expression) on the x-axis and log2 fold change on the y-axis. Points are colored red if they are significantly DE (FDR <= 0.05 and abs(log2FC) >= 1.0), and gray otherwise. Horizontal reference lines are drawn at log2FC = 0 (no change) and log2FC = +/-1.0.

For the Cells_vs_Organs comparison, the log2FC is negated to maintain the convention that positive values = upregulated in the focal group.

### Interpretation
Significant DE miRNAs scattered evenly across expression levels suggest that the differential expression signal is not driven by expression-level artifacts. A funnel shape (more significant hits at low expression) can indicate noise-driven false positives among lowly expressed miRNAs.

### Output
- Six MA plot HTML files (one per comparison).

## Note on the Cells_vs_Organs Comparison

In the source data, the Cells_vs_Organs comparison is structured with group1 = Organs (all five tissue types) and group2 = Cells (4T1), which is the reverse of the naming convention. A positive log2FC in the raw data therefore indicates higher expression in Organs, not in Cells. The pipeline corrects this by negating the log2FC for this comparison in all downstream analyses (biomarker identification, volcano plots, and MA plots), so that positive log2FC consistently indicates upregulation in the focal group across all six comparisons.
